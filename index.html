Pocket Agent

A free, private AI assistant that runs on your own computer (Mac and Windows). No account, no subscription, and your chats stay on your device.

What it does

  • Finds, sorts and organizes files and folders on your computer.
  • Reads and updates your calendar, including Google Calendar if you connect it, and helps reschedule lessons and appointments.
  • Searches the web, remembers your preferences, and can be reached from your phone through your own private Discord bot.
Google Calendar

If you choose Connect Google Calendar in the app, Pocket Agent uses Google's permission to read and change events in your calendars, only when you ask it to, and asks before changing anything. The connection is stored only on your computer. See the privacy policy.
