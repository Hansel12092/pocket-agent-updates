Privacy Policy

Pocket Agent · Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

Pocket Agent runs on your own computer. The developer does not run any server that receives your chats, files, calendar or contacts, and does not collect, sell or share your data.

What stays on your computer

Your chats, memory, settings, student list, and the sign-in token for Google Calendar are stored only on your computer, in Pocket Agent's settings folder. You can delete them at any time by deleting that folder or disconnecting in Settings.

Google Calendar

  • If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Pocket Agent asks Google for permission to view and edit your calendar events (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar).
  • It uses this only to do what you ask: show your schedule, add events, and move, rename or cancel events. It asks you before changing anything.
  • Calendar data goes directly between your computer and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Pocket Agent's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Pocket Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you choose to use

  • The AI model runs on your computer (through Ollama) unless you choose an online model in Settings.
  • Web search: when you ask it to search, your search words are sent to public search engines.
  • Discord: if you set up phone access, your messages to your bot go through Discord under Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Updates: the app checks a public file on GitHub for new versions. This sends no personal data.
